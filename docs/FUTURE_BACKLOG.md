# BubbleShot - Future Feature Backlog

The following concepts and enhancements are intentionally excluded from the initial 7-phase scope to preserve strict focus, determinism, and core quality. They are cataloged here for post-launch consideration:

---

## 1. Additional Special Ball Types
- **Lightning Ball**: Clears an entire horizontal row across the board regardless of color or obstacles.
- **Color Wipe Ball**: Removes all balls of a target color currently existing anywhere on the board.
- **Piercing / Drill Ball**: Penetrates through the first $K$ balls in its trajectory before attaching or expiring.
- **Ice / Freeze Ball**: Temporarily halts the hybrid pressure timer for 10 seconds.

---

## 2. Advanced Game Modes
- **Endless Survival Mode**: Progressive speed ramp with dynamic color insertions and high-score chasing.
- **Puzzle / Limited-Shot Mode**: Static boards requiring clearing the objective within a strictly limited shot allowance (no pressure timer).
- **Boss / Hazard Stages**: Floating obstacles, shifting gravity fields, and shielding nodes.

---

## 3. Visual & Audio Expansions
- **Cosmetic Ball Themes**: Unlockable ball skins (Retro 8-bit, Neon Cyberpunk, Minimalist Pastel) unlocked purely through gameplay milestones.
- **Dynamic Adaptive Soundtrack**: Multi-layered music track that dynamically intensifies as the board approaches the danger line.

---

## 4. Accessibility Enhancements
- **One-Touch Assist Aim**: Aiming trajectory line stays locked on drag release until a dedicated fire button is pressed.
- **Screen Reader Announcements**: VoiceOver / TalkBack announcements for current pressure level and combo status.
